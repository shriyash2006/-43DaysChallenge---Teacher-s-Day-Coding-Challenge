## 🌟 Overview  

Welcome to my **#43DaysChallenge Repository!**  

This challenge is part of the **Mission #DrGViswanathan**, initiated by the **Placement & Training (PAT) Cell, VIT Bhopal University**.  

📅 Starting **July 25, 2025**, this journey is:  
✨ A tribute to teachers  
🚀 A path to excellence  
💰 A step toward the **1 Crore package goal**  

> 💡 *"The dream begins with a teacher who believes in you."*

## 🎯 Objectives  

🔹 **Daily Problem Solving**  
Solve 1 DSA problem every day  

🔹 **Communication Skills**  
Explain solutions clearly (interview-ready mindset)  

🔹 **Consistency**  
Build a habit of showing up every day  

🔹 **Networking**  
Share progress & inspire others
